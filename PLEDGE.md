# The KERF Pledge

Design software should be bought like a hammer, not leased like a car.

These seven promises are written into the code and into this file. The source is open, so
anyone can check that they hold, and anyone can fork KERF if they are ever broken. They apply
to every release published by AKA IND Technologies Inc. and its AI² Lab.

1. **Free, with every tool.**
   No trial, no tiers, no "pro" lock on core features. The same app for a student and a studio.

2. **No account.**
   Open it and work. There is nothing to sign up for, so there is nothing to cancel.

3. **No subscription, no stored card, no auto-renewal.**
   KERF never asks for payment details. If you ever chip in, it is a one-time contribution you
   choose, with no strings (see [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md)).

4. **No tracking.**
   No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party requests. The typefaces ship inside the app. The
   only files KERF fetches are its own (the CAD kernel, and the source archive when you ask for
   it), from the same place the app came from.

5. **Your files are yours.**
   One open, documented JSON format ([FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md)), plus export to PDF, SVG, PNG,
   HTML, STL, OBJ, GLB and STEP. Leaving is always one click. KERF claims no rights over
   anything you make with it.

6. **A licence that keeps it open.**
   AGPL-3.0-or-later: use it, study it, change it, share it. Anyone who hosts a modified KERF
   for others must publish their changes, so nobody can wall it off and rent it back to you.

7. **Works on your machine.**
   Everything runs locally once the page has loaded, including the CAD kernel. Your documents
   are never uploaded anywhere.

## What we will not do

- Hide a feature you already have behind a paywall in a later version.
- Add an "agreement" you must accept to keep opening your own files.
- Make cancelling harder than starting. There is nothing to cancel.
- Sell, share or collect data about you or your work.

## If a hosted copy breaks these promises

KERF is open source, so other people can host it, including modified versions. This pledge
covers releases from AKA IND Technologies Inc. If a copy you are using asks for an account, a
card or tracking consent, it is not one of ours. The licence still guarantees you its source
code: look for **Pledge → Get the source** in the app.
