# Funding

KERF is free and stays free. Nothing in the app is held back for people who pay.

If KERF saves you money and you want to give some back, you can make a **one-time**
contribution here: [FUNDING LINK]

How it works, in full:

- **One-time only.** No subscription, no recurring charge, no "monthly supporter" default.
- **No account and no stored card on our side.** The payment is handled by the platform
  linked above and ends there.
- **No perks.** You get the same KERF as everyone else, and so does everyone who does not pay.
- **No reminders, no nags.** The app will never ask you for money.

What contributions pay for: development time on the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), test hardware,
and keeping the official builds hosted.

Businesses that want an invoice, priority on a roadmap item, or paid help integrating KERF
can write to AKA IND Technologies Inc. at [CONTACT EMAIL]. That work is quoted up front, as a
fixed price, with no ongoing fee.
